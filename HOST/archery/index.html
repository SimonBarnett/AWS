Welcome

Welcome to Laleham Archery Club. We are a friendly group of archers with a great range of skill and experience levels, from very recently trained through to 'old-hands'. If you are interested in learning to shoot, we offer beginner's courses or, if you are already an archer, you are welcome to join our club and then you can shoot on club nights with many other members or whenever you want (light permitting) at our outdoor range.
