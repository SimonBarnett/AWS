What’s a Smart Catalogue?

Our dual AI Smart Catalogue is a custom online shop designed especially for your club, group, or community.
Instead of a one-size-fits-all product catalogue, our system uses smart widget technology to hand-pick products that match your members’ interests.

​

Relevant – Everything is chosen to suit your community’s hobbies, needs, and lifestyle.

Convenient – Your members can browse and buy in one place, without searching all over the internet.

Supportive – Every purchase helps fund your community, because part of the sale goes back to your group.

​

It’s like having a store that knows your members and supports your cause - automatically.
